The experiment

Two original sparse neural policies: Astra-directed, with Sol implementation assistance and an Astra-authored strategic revision. A single feed-forward evaluation ranks legal moves from the current position: no game-tree search, opening book, training, or external engine during play.

These are authored neural chess heuristics, not learned intelligence. Larger does not mean stronger. Neither policy has an established Elo; grandmaster strength is not established.

Rules & clock conventions

Legal moves, checkmate, stalemate and insufficient material use chess.js. Threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule are automatically drawn here instead of requiring a claim. Clocks include all thinking and background-tab time; increment is awarded only after a move made before flag fall. A timeout is recorded as a loss unless the whole position has insufficient material; this is a practical convention, not full tournament adjudication. Imported FEN positions begin with fresh clocks.
