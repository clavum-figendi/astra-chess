About Chess Lab

Opening moves use a weighted, seeded book when enabled. Pocket/Atlas use sparse neural policies; DeepMind 9M scores legal state/action pairs in batches. Neither performs game-tree search. Everything runs on your device.

Rules & clock conventions

Legal moves and terminal positions use chess.js. Threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule are drawn automatically. Thinking and background-tab time count against the clock; increment follows a move made before flag fall. Timeout is a loss unless the whole position has insufficient material. Imported positions use fresh clocks and do not include earlier repetition history. Inference has a 30-second safety timeout, including Unlimited games.
