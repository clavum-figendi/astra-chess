The experiment

Four original sparse neural policies: the preserved Astra-directed, Sol-assisted base models, plus larger Astra-authored expansions. A single feed-forward evaluation ranks legal moves from the current position: no game-tree search, opening book, training, or external engine during play.

Plus weights add fixed king-escape and terminal geometry in king + queen/rook versus bare king endings. This is not a general mate solver. These are authored chess heuristics, not learned intelligence. Larger does not automatically mean stronger; no model has an established Elo.

Rules & clock conventions

Legal moves, checkmate, stalemate and insufficient material use chess.js. Threefold repetition and the fifty-move rule are automatically drawn here instead of requiring a claim. Clocks include all thinking and background-tab time; increment is awarded only after a move made before flag fall. A timeout is recorded as a loss unless the whole position has insufficient material; this is a practical convention, not full tournament adjudication. Imported FEN positions begin with fresh clocks.

The weights are the instrument.

All four policies work in the browser and laptop runtime. Only the selected model is loaded. Start with Pocket on small devices; try Pocket+ or Atlas+ to compare the expanded circuits.

Larger sizes: proposed, not built.

These are next research budgets, not selectable or downloadable models. Added bytes must carry new circuitry and earn their cost on fresh tests.

Browser Max · 64–96 MB

Desktop-browser target: pin-aware support, overloaded-defender warnings and broader mating-net patterns. Reserve roughly 320–416 MB working memory as a planning allowance. Phone suitability and latency are unmeasured.

Laptop Pro · 128–256 MB

Target an 8 GB+ consumer laptop: more candidate-conditioned tactical relationships, pawn races/opposition and additional narrowly validated endgame specialists. Planning allowance: 512–896 MB working memory. An optimized evaluator/compiler may be needed.

Laptop Lab · 256–512 MB

Optional research tier for 16 GB+ laptops only if Pro shows worthwhile gains. Planning allowance: 896–1,664 MB working memory. Requires chunked assets and validated format-bound changes, not just a larger existing file.

Memory estimates use 3 × complete file size + 128 MB of headroom; they are not measurements or guarantees. No future Elo or strength is promised.
